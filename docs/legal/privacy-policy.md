## 1. What The Talk Ahead does

The Talk Ahead is an AI-powered rehearsal tool for workplace conversations. It lets you practise curated or personal workplace situations by voice, interact with an AI-generated counterpart, rewind moments, and receive an AI-generated debrief and preparation plan.

The person you rehearse with, their reactions, inferred motivations, feedback, and coaching are simulated. They may not reflect a real person’s beliefs or likely behaviour.

## 2. Privacy summary

- The Talk Ahead does not currently require an account or profile.
- Saved sessions, transcripts, ratings, and debriefs are primarily stored locally on your device.
- Your voice audio is temporarily recorded and sent to OpenAI for transcription. We do not intentionally retain audio as part of a saved session.
- Transcripts, scenario details, and conversation state are sent to OpenAI to generate responses, speech, and coaching.
- RevenueCat and the applicable app store process purchase and subscription information.
- We do not currently sell personal information, display advertising, or use app activity for cross-context behavioural advertising.
- Use aliases and do not provide unnecessary confidential or sensitive workplace information.

## 4. Voice recordings and transcription

When you start recording, The Talk Ahead requests microphone access. Recording begins only after you take an action to start it.

Your voice is:

1. Captured temporarily on your device.
2. Written to a temporary audio file.
3. Sent through our backend to OpenAI for transcription.
4. Converted into text.
5. Removed from temporary device storage where possible after processing.

The Talk Ahead does not intentionally store your voice recording in its session database. A temporary cache file could remain until later device or app cleanup if deletion is interrupted or fails.

The resulting transcript is stored locally as part of the rehearsal session and is used for AI-generated conversation and coaching.

Audio may pass through our hosting infrastructure and OpenAI’s systems. Provider-side retention for security or abuse monitoring is governed by their policies and cannot be controlled solely by deleting the local file.

## 5. AI processing

The Talk Ahead uses OpenAI for:

- Voice transcription.
- Simulated counterpart responses.
- AI-generated counterpart speech.
- Scenario preparation and normalisation.
- Debriefs, observations, and suggested approaches.
- Word timing used to display spoken captions.

Depending on the feature, OpenAI may receive temporary voice audio, transcripts, scenario details, observable workplace facts, counterpart aliases, desired changes, feared responses, relationship context, and conversation state.

Our OpenAI Responses API requests are configured not to create persistent application state through the request’s `store` setting. This does not necessarily prevent provider security or abuse-monitoring retention.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train or improve its models unless the API customer explicitly opts in. Retention varies by endpoint and account configuration. See [OpenAI’s API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

We do not use your rehearsal content to train models operated by The Talk Ahead.

## 6. Information about other people

Your rehearsal may involve an employee, colleague, manager, client, or stakeholder. You should:

- Use an alias rather than the person’s real name.
- Include only information needed to practise the conversation.
- Use observable facts rather than speculation.
- Avoid confidential employer information and trade secrets.
- Avoid medical, disability, protected-class, disciplinary, financial, or other sensitive information.
- Obtain any authority or permission required before submitting information about another person.

The Talk Ahead is not designed to store official employment records. You are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis and appropriate authority to submit third-party information.

## 7. Local storage

The Talk Ahead stores session details, text transcripts, AI messages, rewind branches, conversation state, readiness ratings, debriefs, plans, and timestamps locally on your device.

The local database is protected by your app sandbox and device security settings. The Talk Ahead does not currently provide cloud synchronisation or end-to-end encryption of its local database.

Depending on your operating-system and backup settings, app data may be included in a device backup controlled by Apple, Google, or your device provider.

Local information may be lost if you delete a session, uninstall the app, clear app data, lose or replace your device, or experience storage failure.

## 8. Purchases and RevenueCat

RevenueCat is initialised when the app starts and is used to manage purchases, subscription status, entitlement access, offerings, and purchase restoration.

RevenueCat may receive:

- An anonymous app-user identifier.
- Device and operating-system information.
- Purchase history and transaction status.
- App-store receipts or purchase tokens.
- Last-seen and entitlement information.
- Limited onboarding values used to personalise a paywall, such as a conversation category or predefined commitment focus.

We do not send voice recordings or complete rehearsal transcripts to RevenueCat.

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your complete payment-card details. See the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and the privacy terms of the applicable app store.

## 11. Retention and deletion

- Temporary user audio is removed from device cache where possible after transcription.
- Temporary AI-generated speech audio is removed where possible after playback or cleanup.
- Local sessions, transcripts, ratings, and debriefs remain until you delete the session or clear app data.
- Onboarding and preference values remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
- RevenueCat and the app stores retain purchase information under their own obligations.

- Support and legal records are retained only as reasonably necessary or legally required.

Deleting a local session does not necessarily delete security or abuse-monitoring logs already held by a provider.
